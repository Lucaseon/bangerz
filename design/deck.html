The problem

The venue wants its deposit before the first ticket is even sold

A student association books a night, fronts the venue, sound and crew before a single euro of ticket revenue exists. The gap between committing to the event and collecting from it is where every small organiser gets stuck.

Cash flow

Costs land first. Ticket revenue lands after doors open.

Who lends today

Nobody, formally — a personal card, a favour, or the event doesn't happen.

What's missing

A way for the association's own community to front the money and get paid back — auditably.

The idea

One night's lifecycle is already a closed-ended vault

Subscription is the presale. Investment is the night itself, running on borrowed capital. Redemption is the morning after, when ticket revenue pays everyone back. XLS-65's three phases aren't a metaphor here — they're the literal event calendar.

How it works

Four roles, one vault

Lenders

The community

Deposit XRP via VaultDeposit, receive vault shares (MPT). Withdraw principal + yield at redemption.

Broker

bangerz

Posts first-loss cover via LoanBrokerCoverDeposit — absorbs losses before any lender does.

Borrower

The organiser

Draws the principal through a double-signed LoanSet, repays from ticket revenue via LoanPay.

Vault

The pseudo-account

Holds nothing itself decision-wise — a ledger object custodies the assets and mints/burns the shares.

Friction, 1 of 3 — the one that cost the most time

LoanPay fails silently unless you already know a flag exists

LoanPay tecEXPIRED

Once currentTime ≥ NextPaymentDueDate, the transaction fails unless Flags: tfLoanLatePayment is set. GracePeriod does not extend the payment window — it only delays when the broker can call impair/default. Neither the error message nor the xrpl.org reference pages say this; only XLS-66 §3.11.4.2 does.

Fix: always compute lateness against the live due date and set the flag — implemented in payScheduled().

Friction, 2 of 3

Two more codes that don't point at their own cause

LoanPay, late-flaggedtecTOO_SOON

Failed 8 seconds past the due date, flag already set. A ~20s margin past due made it succeed. Not in the documented failure list.

LoanSet, 2nd brokertecNO_PERMISSION

Identical parameters to a working LoanSet elsewhere: correct owner roles, cover at threshold, no freeze flags. Reproducible, still unexplained.

Both logged with full repro to the event organiser via the DevEx capture hook.

bangerz07 / 10
Friction, 3 of 3 — a wrong mental model, corrected

tfVaultDonation doesn't exist

Secondary planning material for this event called a tfVaultDonation flag on VaultDeposit mandatory for yield — "the number one pitfall to avoid."

VaultDepositChecked against xrpl.org

xrpl.org's own reference page states it plainly: "There are no flags defined for VaultDeposit transactions." Confirmed absent from the XLS-65 spec and from ripple-binary-codec's definitions too.

The real mechanism: LoanPay repayment raises AssetsTotal directly — cash-basis interest, no donation step required.
